# Royaltree Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how Royaltree (managed by Inkside Digital) collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

## 2. Data We Collect
### a. Data You Provide
- Email (via Google Sign-In) — for authentication
- Name — for personalization
- Profile photo — optional, from Google

### b. Automatically Collected
- Device ID — fraud prevention
- Activity logs — ads watched, missions completed
- Approximate location (via IP) — regional ads
- Crash logs — bug fixing (Firebase Crashlytics)

### c. From Third Parties
- Google AdMob — ad ID, ad interactions
- Firebase — analytics, authentication, Firestore

## 3. How We Use Data
- Provide services (auth, reward, withdrawal)
- Prevent fraud
- Show relevant ads (via AdMob)
- Improve app quality
- Comply with legal obligations

We DO NOT sell your personal data.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing
- Google Firebase
- Google AdMob
- Payment providers (only if you withdraw)

All partners are contractually bound to protect your data.

## 5. Data Security
- Encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Firestore Security Rules restrict access
- SSV (Server-Side Verification)
- Internal access restricted

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)
- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete account and data
- Withdraw consent anytime
- File complaint

Contact support@inkside.digital.

## 7. Data Retention
- Active account: while active
- After deletion: within 30 days
- Activity logs: max 90 days

## 8. Children
Not intended for children under 13. Accounts deleted if discovered.

## 9. Cookies & Similar
App uses DataStore for preferences. AdMob uses Advertising ID (resettable).

## 10. Changes
Policy may change. Material changes notified via app/email.
